Frequently asked, plainly answered

Is Cadence the same as Twilio or Vapi?

No. Twilio gives you telephony. Vapi gives you a managed voice model. Cadence sits on top and gives you CRM-native voice companions — sync contacts into the call, sync outcomes back to the CRM.

Which CRMs are supported?

GoHighLevel and Microsoft Dynamics 365 at launch. HubSpot + Pipedrive + Salesforce on the roadmap. The integration layer is pluggable — adding a new CRM is a 1–2 week build.

Do you make clinical claims?

No. Cadence is a programmable voice companion platform for general use — customer outreach, follow-ups, lead qualification, language practice. Not for clinical, treatment, or caregiver use cases.

How long does setup take?

Free tier: 10 minutes. Pro: a few hours including OAuth flow and CRM mapping. Enterprise: depends on data residency + SSO + audit requirements.

What's the data residency story?

US data in us-east-1 by default. EU + AU regions available on Enterprise. Audio is encrypted at rest. Transcripts can be set to ephemeral.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No annual contracts on Free or Pro. Enterprise contracts are negotiable.
